 # Paikon Plus — séquence à valider

Produit fictif, coque graphite fumée translucide, sticks symétriques, éclairage cyan discret, quatre palettes arrière. La planche de référence fixe son identité. Le storyboard présente les quatre étapes clés.

## Film source et scroll

Un film source continu de 15 secondes sera converti en images WebP optimisées. Il ne sera jamais lu automatiquement sur la page. La position du scroll sélectionnera directement l'image correspondante, dans les deux sens. Le menu de jeu final sera composé en interface, pas généré dans la vidéo.

## Découpage

- **0–30 % / 0–4,5 s :** vue plongeante, LEDs éteintes, approche lente de la caméra.
- **30–60 % / 4,5–9 s :** montée graduelle des LEDs sous les boutons translucides, lumière interne qui révèle la matière.
- **60–90 % / 9–13,5 s :** coque qui se soulève, révélation en couches des modules Hall, moteur haptique, électronique et mécanismes de gâchettes. Les pièces restent alignées avec leur emplacement.
- **90–100 % / 13,5–15 s :** réassemblage et rotation vers l'arrière, grips et palettes visibles. Fondu piloté par le scroll vers un écran d'accueil de jeu fictif.

## Prompt complet du film source

SCENE: One continuous engineering product reveal of the single fictional graphite translucent gaming controller shown in the reference product chart. REFERENCES: product-chart, storyboard. FIRST FRAME: centered overhead three-quarter view, controller powered off, full product visible on a near-black seamless studio stage. FORMAT: single continuous 15-second take, no hard cuts. OUTPUT: landscape 16:9, 720p. OPTICS: 50mm product lens, entire controller sharp, gently softened background. CAMERA: machine-smooth slow approach followed by a controlled arc, product remains centered with generous negative space.

ACTION [0.0–4.5s]: controller remains powered off while camera slowly approaches and lowers from overhead toward a three-quarter front angle. [4.5–9.0s]: cyan LED light slowly intensifies inside joystick collars and translucent action buttons, revealing internal details progressively, no flashing. [9.0–13.5s]: smoked upper shell rises slowly, revealing two Hall joystick sensor modules, green PCB, copper-wound haptic motors and shoulder trigger resistance gears; all parts separate into mechanically aligned exploded layers, camera arcs gently so each part is readable. [13.5–15.0s]: components return to their exact assembled positions as controller rotates smoothly to rear three-quarter view, showing stippled grip texture and four rear programmable paddles. End on the rear view. Game-menu fade is added later in the web interface, not in the film.

PRODUCT INTEGRITY: shape, proportions, materials and component arrangement match product-chart; small label remains unreadable; no competing logos. LIGHTING: broad soft 5600K key from upper left, gentle white rim, matte surfaces absorb light, smoked transparent shell refracts, copper coils retain realistic metallic highlights. GRADE: Cold premium grade: gunmetal and black, deep blacks, controlled specular roll, cyan practical LED accent only, minimal grain, not oversaturated, not teal-orange. AUDIO: no voice or music needed; output audio discarded for the image sequence. POSITIVE LOCKS: exactly one controller throughout; components remain aligned with their actual sockets; LEDs remain off until 4.5 seconds and stay lit afterward; exploded parts move only during the specified reveal and reassembly. NEGATIVE: no text overlays, no invented logos, no duplicate controllers, no shape morphing, no strobe, no excessive neon, no sudden camera cuts.

## Interface prévue

Hero éditorial et séquence épinglée, navigation par chapitres, indicateur de progression, caractéristiques et détails de fabrication. Responsive desktop/mobile. Aucun achat ni collecte d'informations personnelles. Produit présenté explicitement comme concept de démonstration.
